Our Mission

To advance humanity's understanding and exploration of the Universe through the convergence of astronomy, astrophysics, cosmology, mathematics, artificial intelligence and advanced computing—while developing intelligent technologies for space missions, astronomical discovery and the long-term advancement of a spacefaring civilization.

01 / DISCOVER

Understand the Universe

Study the birth and evolution of cosmic structure—from the early Universe to stars, galaxies, planetary systems and black holes.

02 / ENGINEER

Build Space Intelligence

Create resilient AI-enabled software for telemetry, mission operations, scientific data processing and autonomous decision support.

03 / ADVANCE

Expand Human Capability

Explore how science, computation and intelligent systems can help humanity progress toward a durable spacefaring future.

Fundamental Research

Our long-horizon research vision spans observational astronomy, theoretical astrophysics, cosmology, mathematical physics and the search for life and intelligence beyond Earth.

COSMOLOGY

Origins of the Universe

Big Bang cosmology, cosmic inflation, nucleosynthesis, the cosmic microwave background, structure formation and cosmic evolution.

GRAVITY

Black Holes

Stellar, intermediate and supermassive black holes; horizons, accretion, relativistic jets, Hawking radiation and the information problem.

STELLAR

Stars, Nebulae, Gas & Dust

Molecular clouds, stellar birth and evolution, emission/reflection/dark nebulae, supernova remnants and the interstellar medium.

EXTRAGALACTIC

Galaxies Beyond the Milky Way

Galaxy formation and evolution, gas and dust, active galactic nuclei, quasars, mergers, clusters and large-scale structure.

PLANETARY

Exoplanets & Planetary Systems

Planet formation, protoplanetary disks, orbital systems, atmospheric characterization, habitability and planetary evolution.

RADIATIVE

Multi-Wavelength Astronomy

Radio through gamma-ray astronomy, spectroscopy and multimessenger approaches combining photons, gravitational waves and neutrinos.

ASTROBIOLOGY

Life & Technosignatures

Evidence-driven searches for biosignatures and technosignatures, alongside rigorous study of the Fermi paradox and SETI questions.

FOUNDATIONS

Multiverse & Quantum Cosmology

Theoretical study of eternal inflation, bubble universes, many-worlds, the string landscape and mathematical models of possible cosmological structures.

MATHEMATICS

Mathematical Foundations

Algebra, geometry, trigonometry, calculus, differential equations, numerical methods and mathematical physics—from classical mechanics to relativity.

Scientific principle: HARK COSMOS LABS distinguishes established observation from active hypothesis and speculative theory. Multiverse models, extraterrestrial intelligence and related foundational questions are presented as research questions—not established discoveries.

Civilization Futures

Beyond near-term space software, we are interested in the long arc of technological civilization: the Fermi paradox, SETI, technosignatures, artificial intelligence, planetary resilience and the Kardashev framework for thinking about civilization-scale energy and capability.

TYPE I

Planetary Capability

Advanced computation, energy systems, planetary science and technologies that strengthen civilization on Earth.

TYPE II

Stellar Horizons

Long-term questions around interplanetary and interstellar expansion, autonomous systems and detectable stellar-scale engineering.

TYPE III

Galactic Questions

Theoretical exploration of mature technological civilizations, observational signatures and humanity's place in the cosmic story.
